# Disputed items

Nothing in this task was adjudicated after a model was run. No Jev output had been observed when the strata were locked.

High-entropy items are the hard stratum on purpose. They are not removed, relabelled, or treated as annotator error. The 100-annotator distribution is the label. The majority is only the hard-scoring target; soft scoring uses the full distribution.

The middle entropy band (between the 25th and 75th percentiles) is excluded from the primary contrast. That exclusion is a pre-registered design choice, not a dispute about individual items.

Paraphrase rows keep the source item's annotator distribution. They are a wording control, not a new gold label.
