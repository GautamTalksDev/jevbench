## ECE definition (pinned)

**Top-label ECE** is primary:

- Multiclass: `conf = max p_k`, `correct = 1[argmax == y]`
- Binary Noul: `pred = 1[p >= 0.5]`, `conf = max(p, 1-p)`, `correct = 1[pred == y]`
- Bin confidence = **mean** of `conf_i` in the bin (not the midpoint)
- Empty bins contribute zero

This is **not** classic binary reliability of `P(y=1)` against the label.
`netcal.metrics.ECE.measure(p, y)` on raw binary probs implements that classic
variant. We cross-check netcal on our `(conf, correct)` arrays so the
agreement test validates binning arithmetic, not a silent definition swap.

## Bias trap (ΔECE)

ECE is biased upward in finite samples; bias grows as `n` shrinks. Comparing
hard vs easy with unequal `n` can produce positive ΔECE from sample size alone.
Controls:

1. Equal `n` per stratum (`subsample_to_equal_n` + hard guard in `delta_ece`)
2. Null simulation in `tests/test_delta_ece.py` — calibrated strata → CI crosses 0
3. Report `null_delta_ece_band(...)` in methods

### Coverage (Prompt F2)

Null FPR at the operating n over 5000 trials (`results/coverage.json`):

- Percentile bootstrap: empirical FPR ≈ 0.079 (coverage ≈ 92.1%)
- BCa: empirical FPR ≈ 0.112 (coverage ≈ 88.8%)

Neither reaches nominal 0.05. **Do not claim 95%.** Report both intervals;
percentile is closer to nominal and is the current primary.

### Power under noise (Prompt F1)

Required n is chosen at the pessimistic corner
(`label_noise=0.05`, `difficulty_sd=0.10`), not the clean simulator.
At that corner, power ≥ 0.80 first at **n=750** per stratum — above the
labelling ceiling of 500. **Do not proceed underpowered**; pre-specify a
larger detectable effect size or raise labelling capacity before any paid run.

### Composite tracks power curve (Amendment 9 / PROMPT R)

The Amendment 9 **tracks** gate is `corrected ΔECE ≥ 0.09 AND p < 0.05`.
At a true effect of exactly 0.09, requiring the point estimate to clear 0.09
is a median split — composite power ≈ **0.52** by construction
(`results/verdict_size.json`). Parametric p-value power at that effect remains
≈ 1.0. Do not claim 80% power for the composite gate at the boundary.

## What is checked

1. **Calibrated fixture** (Bernoulli draws from known probs): our uniform
   top-label ECE matches netcal on identical `(conf, correct)` to `1e-9`.
2. **Miscalibrated fixture** (constant 0.95 vs 20% positives): same agreement.
3. **Classic vs top-label divergence** documented on a low-probability fixture.
4. **Brier** matches `sklearn.metrics.brier_score_loss` to `1e-9`.
5. **ΔECE** equal-n guard, stratified two-sample bootstrap, null band.

## Guard

Calibration entry points raise `TypeError` if a `ConfidenceArray` (Jev
sharpness) or a `confidence=` kwarg is passed.
